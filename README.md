1. Project Name: MentorLagbe

2. Purpose:

MentorLagbe is an online tutoring and mentorship platform that connects students with verified tutors. It allows students to find subject-wise tuition batches or one-to-one mentors based on their educational needs, budget, and preferred learning mode (online or offline).

5. Key Features:

A. Student, Tutor, and Admin registration and login.

B. Google authentication option.

C. Browse and search tuition listings.

D. Filter tuitions by subject, class, budget, and learning mode.

E. Tutors can create and manage tuition batches.

F. Students can apply for tuition opportunities.

G. Admin approval and management of tuition listings.

H. Online and offline mentorship support.

I. Student and tutor dashboards.

J. Free and Pro subscription plans.

K. Stripe payment integration for subscriptions.

L. Platform statistics and featured tuition listings.
